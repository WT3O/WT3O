# Nicholas B. Proy

**Attorney | Technologist | Author**

I am an attorney, technologist, and author whose work spans law, artificial intelligence, information verification, software development, privacy, and technical systems.

While my professional work and personal projects may involve different subjects, they share a consistent methodology: understand the underlying structure, identify the variables that matter, question assumptions, verify results, and make the outcome useful.

## Technical Interests and Projects

My work includes browser-based applications, structured reference databases, technical calculators, and experimental tools involving:

- **AI and Verification Coding:** Human-directed software development, source verification, testing, version control, and preserving known-good baselines.
- **Structured Data and Metadata:** Data normalization, provenance, research workflows, and information validation.
- **Amateur Radio and RF Engineering:** Antenna calculations, repeaters, transceivers, and technical reference tools.
- **Analog Audio and Vinyl:** Turntable setup, phono cartridges, audio measurement, and equipment comparison.
- **Cybersecurity and Privacy:** Understanding technical risks, protecting information, and evaluating claims critically.

Many of my projects are published as self-contained, browser-based tools through the WT3O Hobby Workshop.

## Writing and Publications

My writing has appeared in *2600: The Hacker Quarterly* and the *Maryland Bar Journal*, addressing AI verification, software development, privacy, legal technology, and professional judgment.

I am also the author of *The Cognitive Craftsman® System*, a music-centered framework for structured self-reflection that draws on systems thinking, observation, and intentional practice.

## My Approach

I am particularly interested in the distinction between making something work and understanding **why it works, whether its underlying information is accurate, and how its results can be independently verified**.

I use AI as an instrument for exploration and productivity, not as a substitute for critical thinking, technical validation, or human judgment.
